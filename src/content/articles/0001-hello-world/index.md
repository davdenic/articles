Hi, I'm David — a full-stack developer in Switzerland, working with TYPO3 and the web for over two decades. This is where I think out loud about AI-assisted software development and the engineering practices that make it actually work. Short, practical notes, no hype.

Today I lead web development at a Swiss TYPO3 agency, where I'm bringing AI into everyday engineering — building Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers and shared CI tooling so assistants like Claude can actually help ship reliable code.

Before that, I spent years as a senior full-stack developer and Scrum Master at a Bern non-profit — running a large TYPO3 platform and migrating a monolithic PHP app to a modular Odoo/FastAPI architecture. Earlier still, I founded and ran my own web agency in Italy: hundreds of TYPO3 and e-commerce projects.

I'm part of the TYPO3 community — I've spoken at T3CON, shared talks on things like frontend performance, and I'm a regular at TYPO3 Camp Schweiz.
