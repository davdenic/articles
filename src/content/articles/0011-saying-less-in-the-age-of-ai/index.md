A personal reflection on web communication:

Now that AI makes it possible for everyone to write polished, complex, content-rich texts, I can't help noticing a strange kind of flattening.

So many things are well written — and yet, somehow, they all sound like they were written by the same person.

Maybe this is also why I'm increasingly drawn to the opposite: saying less, cutting through the noise, and getting to the root of what we actually want to communicate.

In a world where everyone can write more, perhaps the real skill is knowing what doesn't need to be said.
